Your food decides your workout

Nutrika counts calories like any other app, but it also trains with you. And it never asks you to make up for what you ate.

SCAN Ai

Photo, voice or search

Snap the plate, pick a photo from your gallery, describe it out loud or search the catalogue. Fix anything that looks off and the total adjusts on its own.

The coach

The session of the day

The coach reads what you have eaten today and suggests the session: the long routine if you ate more, a shorter one if you ate less.

Gym

Programs with video

Four programs that repeat in a cycle, plus standalone workouts by muscle group. Every exercise has a video, and the weight of each set is saved for next time.

HIIT

10 to 20 minutes at home

Video-guided routines with warm-up and cool-down. Swap, remove or add exercises whenever you want.

Schedule

The days you train

Pick the days and the time, and the app reminds you. If you already trained that day, it stays quiet.

Progress

Weight, streak and reports

Weight chart, daily calorie average, weekly budget and a PDF report you can share with your nutritionist.

Eating well and training go together

You can train every day, but if your food does not follow, progress stalls. And the other way round: eating perfectly without moving gets you nowhere either. That is why Nutrika puts both in a single app, so what you eat and what you train pull in the same direction.

You log your meals in seconds and the coach suggests the session that fits that day. No juggling two apps and no doing the math: every week you move towards your goal.

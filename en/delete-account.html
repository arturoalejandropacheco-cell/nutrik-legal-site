Delete your account

Nutrika (developed by Drift Studio) lets you permanently delete your account and all your data.

From the app

  1. Open Nutrika and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap "Delete account permanently".
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Don't have the app installed?

Write to us at admin@driftstudio.tech from the email address associated with your account, stating that you want to delete it. We will process your request within a maximum of 30 days.

What data is deleted

Deleting your account permanently and irreversibly erases: your profile, meal logs, weight records, goals, reports, and all information associated with your account.

What data is retained

We do not retain personal data after deletion. Subscription billing records that the law requires to be kept remain with the payment provider (Google Play or Apple) under their own policies — not on our servers.

⚠️ This action is irreversible: you will lose all your logs, reports, and progress.

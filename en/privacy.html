1. Introduction

Welcome to Nutrika. This Privacy Policy describes how Arturo Pacheco (hereinafter, "Nutrika", "we", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application Nutrika (the "App").

Nutrika is a nutrition-tracking application designed for the Latin American market that lets you log your meals, analyze food photos with artificial intelligence, track your weight and goals, and generate PDF reports of your progress.

By using Nutrika, you accept the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide to us

  • Account data: name, email address, password (encrypted).
  • Physical profile data: gender, date of birth, height, current weight, target weight.
  • Lifestyle data: activity level, diet type, nutrition goals, cooking preferences.
  • Nutrition data: logged meals, calories consumed, macronutrients (protein, carbohydrates, fat), food photos.
  • Progress data: weight records over time, adherence to your plan.
  • Communications: any message you send us when you contact us.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device data: model, operating system, unique device identifiers, language, time zone.
  • Usage data: interactions with the App, features used, session time, technical errors.
  • Subscription data: your plan status (free or Nutrika Pro), purchase and renewal dates (via Apple and RevenueCat).

2.3 Food photos

When you take a photo of your food, the image is sent to Google Gemini AI for nutritional analysis. Nutrika does not permanently store the photos on its servers — we only save the results of the analysis (food name, portion, calories, macros). The image is processed and then discarded.

3. How we use your information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining the Nutrika service.
  • Calculating your personalized nutrition plan based on your physical data and goals.
  • Analyzing food photos using artificial intelligence.
  • Generating PDF reports of your progress that we can send you by email.
  • Sending local notifications to remind you to log your meals.
  • Processing and managing your Nutrika Pro subscription.
  • Improving the App and developing new features.
  • Responding to your inquiries and providing support.
  • Complying with legal obligations.
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring the security of the service.

4. Third-party services

Nutrika uses the following third-party services to operate. Each one has its own privacy policy:

4.1 Supabase (Database and authentication)

We store your account, profile, and nutrition-log data in Supabase. Supabase's privacy policy.

4.2 Google Gemini AI (Image analysis)

Food photos are sent to Google Gemini for nutritional analysis. Google processes the images temporarily. Google's privacy policy.

4.3 RevenueCat (Subscription management)

RevenueCat manages your Nutrika Pro subscription in coordination with Apple. RevenueCat's privacy policy.

4.4 Apple (Authentication and payments)

If you use "Sign in with Apple" or purchase a subscription, Apple processes that data under its own policy. Apple's privacy policy.

4.5 Resend (Email delivery)

We use Resend to email you PDF reports when you request them. Resend's privacy policy.

5. How we share your information

Nutrika does not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share your information under the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers essential to operating the App (listed above).
  • With your explicit consent when you use features such as the referral program.
  • When legally required if a competent authority compels us to by court order.
  • To protect rights when necessary to prevent fraud or protect users' safety.
  • In the event of a merger or acquisition, if Nutrika is acquired, we will notify users at least 30 days in advance.

6. Data security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Password encryption using secure hashing algorithms.
  • Restricted access to personal data, limited to authorized personnel.
  • Periodic security reviews.

However, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data, but we are committed to notifying you in the event of a significant breach affecting your personal information.

7. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to retain it for a longer period (for example, subscription billing records for tax purposes).

8. Your rights

You have the following rights over your personal information:

  • Access: request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your account and data.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured format (JSON).
  • Objection: object to certain processing of your data.
  • Withdraw consent: when processing is based on your consent.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at admin@driftstudio.tech. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

Nutrika is intended for people over 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it immediately. If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us.

10. International transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including the United States and Europe, where our service providers operate. By using Nutrika, you accept this international transfer of data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by email before the changes take effect. The most recent version will always be available on this page.

12. Governing law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Chile, in particular Law No. 19,628 on the Protection of Private Life. If you reside in the European Union, your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) also apply. If you reside in California, your rights under the CCPA also apply.

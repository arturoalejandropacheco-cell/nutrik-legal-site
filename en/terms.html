2. Description of the service

Nutrika is a mobile nutrition-tracking and training application that lets you:

  • Log meals manually, by voice or by photo, analyzed with artificial intelligence.
  • Calculate a personalized nutrition plan based on your physical data and goals.
  • Track your weight, calories, and macronutrients.
  • Train with gym programs and HIIT routines at home, guided by video, and get the session the coach suggests from what you ate that day.
  • Save the weight and the sets of every exercise, and pick the days and times you train to get reminders on your phone.
  • Generate PDF reports of your progress.
  • Receive reminders to log your meals.

  • Starting any exercise program, especially if you have an injury, a cardiovascular or respiratory condition, are pregnant, are over 65, or have not trained in a long time.

About training

Nutrika's gym and HIIT routines are general programs, not an exercise prescription made for you. Nobody supervises your form: the videos show the movement in a general way and do not replace an in-person trainer.

  • Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath or chest discomfort, and seek medical attention.
  • Use loads you can control, and do not train with maximum weights without a spotter.
  • Warm up first, and adapt or skip any exercise you cannot do safely.
  • The workout the coach suggests from what you ate is guidance only: it is never an instruction to compensate for or burn off your food.

Nutrika's calculations are general estimates based on standard formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor, activity factors) and on AI analysis that may contain errors. Do not rely solely on Nutrika to make important decisions about your health.
